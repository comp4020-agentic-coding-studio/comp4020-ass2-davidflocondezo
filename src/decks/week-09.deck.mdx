# Hagiography & Iconography

**No saint declares themselves one.**

---

## Sainthood is conferred, not claimed

A community keeps venerating someone after the fact.

That's exactly how virality works — and exactly why neither can be
manufactured by the person it happens to.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> "You can become venerable. You cannot declare yourself venerated."

---

## What makes veneration repeatable

**The icon** — one compressed, instantly recognisable image, gesture, or
sound that stands in for the whole canonised body of work.

- a saint gets a symbol
- a creator gets a signature bit
- either lets recognition happen in under a second

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## The real test

Not: *is this popular?*

But: *has this earned the specific, durable, instantly-recognisable status
that lets an audience keep recognising and re-venerating it?*
