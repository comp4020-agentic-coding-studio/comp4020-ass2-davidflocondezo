# Glossolalia

**Opacity to outsiders was always the point.**

---

## What speaking in tongues was actually for

Not communicating information outward — marking, audibly, that the speaker
belongs to a community sharing an experience outsiders can't access.

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## Slang your parents can't parse does the identical work

The opacity isn't a side-effect of the language changing too fast to keep up
with. It's the feature that makes the language worth having.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> "Fluency isn't vocabulary. It's what survives the round trip."

---

## The actual fluency test

- translate a passage of slang to plain English
- translate it back
- see what's left — jargon dies here, language survives it

---

{/* _class: centered */}

## Today's practicum

Translate a passage of current slang to plain English, then back, and compare
the two versions.

---

## Bring to next week

Both versions, and a note on what didn't survive the round trip.
