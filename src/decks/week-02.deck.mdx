# Cosmogony

**Something moves everything else, without itself being moved.**

---

## Every cosmogony needs a prime mover

For a feed, that's the ranking system — not a neutral pipe carrying content to
you, but the thing that decided which content existed for you at all.

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## Propitiate the god. Don't argue with the weather.

Rewatch, linger, and follow are the liturgy. The For You Page is the god's
answer.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

## The liturgy of engagement

- **rewatch** — this matters more than I skipped past
- **linger** — even a pause is a vote
- **follow** — a standing offering, not a one-off
