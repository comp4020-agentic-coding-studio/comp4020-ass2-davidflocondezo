# Asceticism

**Training past the point your body says stop.**

---

## Every ascetic discipline shares a structure

1. Find the point where the body says *enough*.
2. Go past it, on purpose.
3. Because something on the other side is worth the discomfort.

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## Fasting works past hunger. Vigil works past sleep.

This week's discipline works past a specific, recognisable moment: eye strain,
a flicker of boredom, a felt "enough" — where a scroll would ordinarily end.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> "A body trained past its first 'enough' isn't broken. It's disciplined."

---

## Discipline, not symptom

That reframing is the whole theological move this week makes.

- **symptom** → something to eliminate
- **discipline** → something to train, deliberately, on purpose

---

{/* _class: centered */}

## Today's practicum

Scroll past your own limit, on purpose, and notice the exact moment the
"enough" feeling arrives.

That moment is the data.

---

## Bring to next week

A record of where your "enough" showed up, and what you did after it.
