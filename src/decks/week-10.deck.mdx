# Prophecy & False Prophets

**Confidence was never what separated the true prophet from the false one.**

---

## What a prophecy actually is

A claim about a crisis or a reveal that hasn't happened yet — judged only
after the fact, against what actually happens.

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## Why false prophecy spreads faster

A true prophecy is constrained by what will actually happen. A false one is
only constrained by what sounds urgent enough to share immediately.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> "Urgency, not evidence, is the variable that predicts spread."

---

## Reading misinformation as prophecy

- urgent, specific, delivered with total confidence
- stated as though the speaker already has privileged access
- judged only by whether the claim survives contact with the event

---

{/* _class: centered */}

## Today's practicum

Trace a claim back through at least two earlier versions to its first
prophecy. Find a true prophecy from the same period to compare it against.
