# Apocrypha & Pseudepigrapha

**A text can be well-written and still not belong in the canon.**

---

## Rejected for provenance, not quality

Apocrypha are attributed to an author who didn't write them. Some are
extraordinary writing. The failure is lineage, not prose.

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## AI slop's exact failure mode

Confident tone, moral clarity, plausible detail — traceable to no author, no
lived experience, no event.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> "A forger has to understand canon well enough to imitate its form
> convincingly."

---

## The actual test this week

Not *can you make slop* — anyone can. It's *can you show your work*.

- which formal features are borrowed
- which provenance is missing
- and whether you can say so precisely
