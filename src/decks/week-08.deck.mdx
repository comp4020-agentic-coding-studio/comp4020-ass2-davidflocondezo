# Homiletics

**A sermon and a rage-bait post are built from the same three parts.**

---

## The shared structure

1. A hook claims attention before the audience knows what's being argued.
2. A provocation produces a felt reaction — outrage, vindication, disbelief.
3. A close reveals it was never trying to prove anything at all.

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## Homiletics, not apologetics

Persuasion toward a response, judged by whether the response happens — not by
whether the claim survives scrutiny afterward.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> "It's judged by its replies and its shares, never by whether it was right."

---

## Building the three parts

- **hook** — a claim with no argument attached yet
- **provocation** — engineered to be felt before it's reasoned about
- **bait-and-switch** — the reveal that the reaction was the point
