# Indulgences

**Relief, sold at the moment of payment.**

---

## Not a purchase of future forgiveness

An indulgence settles an existing debt of guilt without the harder work of
penance.

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## The Shop tab runs the identical transaction

The countdown timer. "Only 2 left." The bundle that makes not-buying feel like
the irrational choice.

All convert one feeling — missing out — into a debt a single payment
discharges.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> "The value is almost entirely in the relief it produces, not the object
> being sold."

---

## Why the interface is built this way

- it doesn't need to be a good deal
- the checkout is built to maximise the moment of payment
- not the object being sold

## Today's practicum

Open the Shop tab of an app you use, right now, for fifteen minutes.
Screenshot every checkout pattern you're shown, and decide live: buy, resist,
or still deciding.
