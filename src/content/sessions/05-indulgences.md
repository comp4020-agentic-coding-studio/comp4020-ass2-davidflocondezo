Bring the app whose Shop tab you find hardest to close without buying
something, and a week's worth of log.

## In the tutorial

An indulgence remits guilt with a payment rather than a change in behaviour;
the Shop tab does the same to FOMO. Brother Yarrow walks through the checkout
patterns — countdowns, "only 2 left," bundling — doing the theological work of
absolution in interface form. The week's log is what turns that into evidence
rather than an anecdote:

1. Every time you open a Shop tab this week, note the pattern it uses on you
   and what you did about it — bought, resisted, or still deciding.
2. Screenshot at least two distinct patterns as you catch them.
3. Tally what you were shown against what you actually spent, and bring the
   gap as a number.

## Afterwards

You leave having named the pattern that got you across a whole week, not one
purchase, and a dollar figure for the difference between the two.
