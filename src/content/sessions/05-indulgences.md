Bring the app whose Shop tab you find hardest to close without buying
something.

## In the tutorial

This tutorial is a live audit of the dark patterns — countdowns, "only 2
left," bundling — that a real Shop tab uses to convert FOMO into a purchase.
Yarrow has you run the audit in the room, on an app you actually use, so each
pattern gets caught as it happens rather than recalled afterward from memory:

1. Open the Shop tab of an app you actually use, and browse it for fifteen
   minutes as if you were about to buy something.
2. Screenshot every checkout pattern you're shown as you hit it — at least
   three.
3. For each screenshot, decide on the spot: buy, resist, or still deciding —
   and if it's "buy," go as far as the checkout screen before backing out.
4. Tally what you were shown against what you'd actually have spent, and
   state the gap as a number.

## Afterwards

You leave having named the pattern that worked on you in real time, not from
memory, and a dollar figure for the gap between what you were shown and what
you'd actually spend.
