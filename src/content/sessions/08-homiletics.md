Bring a rage-bait post you've seen work on you, specifically — recalled live,
nothing prepared in advance.

## In the tutorial

This tutorial has you write and rewrite a rage-bait post using the same
three-part structure — hook, provocation, bait-and-switch — that makes one
actually work. Skein runs the workshop on it twice, not once, ahead of week
9's assessment:

1. Draft a rage-bait post using the three-part structure.
2. Rewrite it, deliberately changing one structural part — a sharper hook, a
   colder provocation, a later reveal — and note what changed and why.
3. Trade your stronger draft with another student's, and take one thing from
   theirs back into yours.

## Afterwards

You leave with a workshopped draft and the vocabulary to say which part is
doing which job, ready for the Rage-Bait Canonization argument in week 9.
