Bring a sentence you say every day, in plain English.

## In the session

Speaking in tongues works as a marker of belonging precisely because it's
unintelligible to anyone outside the community; Gen Alpha slang does the same
job. Brother Yarrow runs the translation drills both ways — plain English into
slang, and back — so the meaning survives the round trip even when the words
don't.

## Afterwards

You leave with a transcript of a real conversation held mostly in this
semester's tongues.
