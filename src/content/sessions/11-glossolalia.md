Bring three sentences you say every day, in plain English.

## In the tutorial

This tutorial tests how much of current slang actually survives being
translated to plain English and back — the real test of fluency. Yarrow runs
the round trip three times over, so the places where meaning breaks down are
the actual data:

1. Translate three plain-English sentences into current slang.
2. Translate each back into plain English without looking at the original.
3. Compare all three round trips, and mark exactly where meaning survived and
   where it didn't.
4. Hold a real conversation with at least one other person, conducted mostly
   in slang, and transcribe it.

## Afterwards

You leave with three round trips, a transcript of a real conversation held
mostly in this semester's tongues, and an account of what plain English can't
do that the slang can.
