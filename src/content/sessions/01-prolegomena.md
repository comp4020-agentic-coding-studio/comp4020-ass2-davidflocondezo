Bring nothing but a phone you already use. This course's lab is your own feed,
and the first tutorial is the only orientation it gets.

## In the tutorial

Dr Sconce lays out the hermeneutics of the shitpost — reading a post for the
work it's actually doing, rather than the work it claims to be doing — and the
one-sentence thesis every later week assumes: that idle scrolling is a practice
worth studying, not a vice waiting to be cured.

The practicum runs the method cold, on your own feed, before you've had time to
perform for it:

1. Open your feed and stop on the first five posts you'd normally scroll past.
2. For each, write one sentence naming the work it's actually doing — marking
   status, converting attention, killing time on purpose — as against whatever
   it presents itself as doing.
3. Post something with no defensible purpose, and write two sentences on what
   work you meant it to do.

## Afterwards

You leave with five readings, one post, and the syllabus's promise that every
week from here trains one further piece of the thesis those readings assumed.
