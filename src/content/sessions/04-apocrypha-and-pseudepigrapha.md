Bring a laptop or phone with access to an AI tool — both drafts get produced
here, live, before they're workshopped against each other.

## In the tutorial

This tutorial has you produce two pieces of AI-generated text and then work
out exactly what gives each one away — the same provenance check from week 3,
run in reverse. Crane has you do it twice, not once, because the second
attempt is where you actually get better at spotting the tell:

1. Produce two pieces of AI slop, aiming for a different canonical marker in
   each — one borrowing scripture's confidence, the other its moral certainty.
2. For each, name the provenance signal that would give it away to a careful
   reader.
3. Trade with another student. Read theirs for the same two things, and revise
   at least one line of your own against what they caught that you didn't.

## Afterwards

You leave with a finished Apocryphal Composition, a second draft's worth of
diagnosis behind it, and a case for why the submitted piece counts as forged
scripture rather than just bad content.
