## In the tutorial

Sainthood is granted after the fact, by a community's veneration, not declared
in advance — virality works the same way. Iconography is what makes the
veneration repeatable: a saint gets a symbol, a creator gets a signature bit,
and either one lets recognition happen in under a second. The tutorial builds
the case for canonization out of two live pieces of evidence rather than one:

1. Track a real creator's icon across at least two platforms or contexts, and
   name what stayed recognisable across the gap.
2. Post (or re-check, if already posted) your week-8 draft, and find the exact
   moment — a comment, a share, a stitch — where it either got canonized or
   visibly didn't.
3. Argue the case for or against your own post's sainthood, using both pieces
   of evidence.
