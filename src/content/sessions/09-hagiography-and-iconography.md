Bring a phone and your week-8 draft — the canonization case gets built here,
on someone else's real history, then applied to yours.

## In the tutorial

This tutorial builds the case for canonization on evidence that already
exists, rather than waiting to see if your own post takes off. Skein has you
study a real creator's icon and a real viral moment first, then apply the
same test to your own week-8 draft:

1. Track a real creator's icon across at least two platforms or contexts,
   live, and name what stayed recognisable across the gap.
2. Find an already-viral post or creator moment — not your own — and locate
   the exact instant in its comment or share history where canonization
   became visible.
3. Apply the same test to your own week-8 draft: argue, from the room,
   whether it clears that bar or falls short. The argument is gradable even
   before the real-world verdict is in.

## Afterwards

You leave with Rage-Bait Canonization submitted: the post, a canonization case
modelled on real evidence found live, and the argument for your own post's
sainthood.
