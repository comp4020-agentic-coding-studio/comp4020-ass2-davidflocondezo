Bring your own For You Page, mid-reshape.

## In the session

Cosmogony asks what set the feed in motion, and answers it the way any creation
myth does — with an unmoved mover. Here it's the ranking system: not neutral
plumbing, but the thing every later doctrine assumes was there first. Brother
Yarrow runs the practicum half, walking through the concrete moves (rewatch,
linger, follow) that actually retrain a feed.

## Afterwards

You leave with a For You Page that's visibly, deliberately different from the
one you brought in, and the vocabulary to say why.
