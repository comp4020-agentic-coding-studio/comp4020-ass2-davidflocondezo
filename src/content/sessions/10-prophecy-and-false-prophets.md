Bring a phone or laptop and be ready to dig — a piece of misinformation you've
seen spread works, or find one live in your feed if nothing comes to mind.

## In the tutorial

Every prophecy is a claim about a coming crisis or reveal, and what makes false
prophecy spread faster isn't that it's more believable — it's that it's built
for higher stakes and less patience than the true version can usually offer.
The practicum traces the whole lineage, not just the endpoint:

1. Start from the version of the claim you first saw, and work backwards
   through at least two earlier, different-sounding versions to the original
   prophecy.
2. Mark where urgency was added at each mutation.
3. Find a true prophecy from the same period, and name what it had — evidence,
   patience, a willingness to be wrong — that the false one skipped.

## Afterwards

You leave with a traced lineage from spread claim back to first prophecy, a
true prophecy for comparison, and the criteria that told them apart.
