Bring whatever's trending the morning of the tutorial — it may not still be
trending by the afternoon.

## In the tutorial

Liturgical calendars recur on a fixed rhythm that gives devotion its shape; the
trend calendar recurs on no fixed rhythm at all, which is the harder discipline
to keep. The tutorial is timed deliberately against whatever's live that week,
so the lesson can't be learned a week late, and the practicum tracks the
window closing in real time:

1. Post into the live trend the moment you identify it, and timestamp it.
2. Record the engagement at posting.
3. Record it again one day later, and again three days later.
4. Plot the three numbers against each other — the decay curve is the
   argument.

## Afterwards

You leave having posted inside a trend's window rather than after it, and with
a three-point record of exactly how fast that window closed.
