> Draft the sermon. Then make the case for its sainthood.

A sermon and a rage-bait post share a structure: a hook that claims attention
before the audience knows the argument, a provocation engineered for reaction
over reflection, and a close that reveals the reaction was the point all
along. This assessment carries the post you drafted in week 8's homiletics
practicum through to its canonization case.

## Part A — the post

Refine the rage-bait post from week 8 into a finished draft, following the
hook / provocation / bait-and-switch structure covered in that week's tutorial.
The medium is your call — a text post, an image, a meme, a mock news
report, whatever else — the only requirement is that it's built as rage-bait.

## Part B — the canonization argument

In 400–600 words, argue for your post's canonization: identify the specific
icon — the one compressed, recognisable image, gesture, or phrase — that would
let it be re-recognised after the fact, and address at least one objection a
sceptical classmate could raise against its staying power.

## What you submit

The finished post and the written argument, submitted together.
