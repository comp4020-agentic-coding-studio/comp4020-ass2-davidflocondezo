Author of the (unpublished, unpublishable) *For You: A Systematic Theology of
the Feed*, and convenor of this course since its first cohort. Dr Sconce holds
that every scroll is a devotional act whether or not the scroller knows it, and
built this degree to give that act a curriculum.

Teaches the connective doctrine — Prolegomena, Cosmogony, Canon, Apocrypha,
Homiletics, Hagiography, Prophecy, and Missiology — and marks every assessment
personally, on the grounds that heresy is easier to spot in your own students'
work than in a rubric.
