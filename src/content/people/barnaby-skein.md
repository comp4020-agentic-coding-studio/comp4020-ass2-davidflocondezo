Has never won an argument by being right, and considers this a point of
professional pride. Runs the weeks where doctrine has to land on someone else
— crafting the hook and the provocation, building the case for a post's
sainthood, and closing the semester by getting an actual convert.

Grading philosophy: a reaction is data. Being unconvincing is the only wrong
answer.
