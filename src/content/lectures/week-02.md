Every cosmogony needs something that moves everything else without itself
being moved. For a feed, that's the ranking system: not a neutral pipe
carrying content to you, but the thing that decided which content existed for
you at all. Nothing you scroll past was inevitable — it was selected, by a
process older than your account and indifferent to your intentions.

Reading it as a prime mover rather than a tool changes what "curating your
feed" means. You aren't picking content directly; you're training the thing
that picks it for you, the way older cosmogonies had you propitiate a god
rather than argue with the weather. Rewatch, linger, and follow are the
liturgy; the For You Page is the god's answer.

## Outline

- the ranking system as unmoved mover, not neutral plumbing
- engagement signals as the liturgy that shapes the god's answer
- curating a feed as training the mover, not picking the content
