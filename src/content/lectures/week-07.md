A liturgical calendar disciplines devotion by making it recur on a fixed
schedule you can prepare for. The trend calendar disciplines the same way, but
without ever telling you the schedule in advance — a sound, a format, or a
phrase becomes the week's feast day with no warning, and stops being one just
as suddenly. Keeping this calendar is harder than keeping a fixed one for
exactly that reason: there's no way to prepare except by attending constantly.

That's also why timing outranks quality here in a way it doesn't in most other
doctrines this course teaches. A well-made post inside a dead trend's window
gets nothing; a rough one inside a live window gets everything. The
liturgical year rewards presence, not craft.
