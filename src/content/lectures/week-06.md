Every ascetic discipline shares a structure: find the point where the body
says stop, and keep going past it on purpose, because something on the other
side is worth more than the comfort you gave up to get there. Fasting works
past hunger. Vigil works past sleep. This week's discipline works past the
specific, recognisable moment — eye strain, a flicker of boredom, a felt
"enough" — where a scroll would ordinarily end.

Treating that as training rather than as a symptom to eliminate is the entire
theological move. A body that's been trained to keep going past its first
"enough" isn't broken; by this course's doctrine, it's disciplined, in exactly
the sense a monk's is.
