# Policies and support

## Late work

Each assessment is due at noon on its listed date. Work submitted within 48
hours of the deadline is accepted with a 5% penalty per day, rounded in the
student's favour; nothing is accepted after 48 hours without an extension
granted in advance. A late Rage-Bait Canonization is, admittedly, thematically
appropriate, and will still lose the 5%.

## Extensions

Email the convenor before the deadline, not after, with what's happened and
how much time you need. Extensions are granted for the reason given, not
negotiated up or down afterward — ask for the time you actually need the
first time.

## Academic integrity

Every doctrine this course teaches is about producing content that reads as
sincere without being verifiable — which makes it the worst possible course
to submit unverifiable work in. Declare any AI assistance you use, the same
way Apocryphal Composition asks you to declare a forgery's borrowed features:
what you used it for, and what you didn't. Undeclared slop submitted as your
own scripture is treated as a straightforward integrity breach, not as an
advanced submission.

## Getting help

Office hours are held weekly; see each teacher's page under
[People](/people/) for contact details. For anything that can't wait for
office hours, email the relevant teacher directly — Dr Sconce for doctrine
and assessment questions, Yarrow for the practicum weeks.
